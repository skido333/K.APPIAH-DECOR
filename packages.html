Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Are prices fixed or can they change?
The prices shown are starting guides. Your final quote depends on your exact measurements, wood species, finish and any modifications. We always provide a detailed, itemised quote before work begins — no hidden costs.
How long does production take?
Typically 2–6 weeks depending on the package and complexity. We'll confirm your exact timeline after consultation and deposit. Full Home Premium packages get priority scheduling.
Can I mix items from different packages?
Absolutely! Packages are guides, not rigid rules. You can mix, match, add or remove items. Just call or message us and we'll build a custom quote around your exact needs.
What is the payment structure?
We require a 50% deposit to commence production, with the remaining 50% due upon delivery and installation. For larger Full Home packages, flexible payment plans can be discussed — call 0247 104 145.
Do you deliver outside Kumasi?
Yes! We deliver across the Ashanti Region and can arrange delivery to other regions for larger orders. Delivery costs outside Kumasi will be quoted transparently upfront.
What does "Premium Wood" pricing mean?
Toggling to "Premium Wood" shows the discounted price for upgrading to hardwood species like mahogany or teak. You save 15% compared to ordering the upgrade separately.

Not Sure Which Package Is Right for You?

Call us for a free, no-obligation consultation. We'll visit your space and recommend the perfect solution.
