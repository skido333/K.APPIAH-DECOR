Common Questions

Before You Reach Out

Quick answers to questions we hear most often.

How much does custom furniture cost?
Prices depend on size, wood type, finish and complexity. Our packages start from GH₵ 3,500 for bedroom sets and GH₵ 4,800 for kitchen cabinets. We always provide a free, itemised quote before any work begins.
How long does it take to complete an order?
Production time is typically 2–6 weeks depending on the product and complexity. After your consultation and deposit, we'll confirm your exact timeline. We always aim to deliver on or before the agreed date.
Do you offer a free consultation?
Yes — absolutely free. You can call us, WhatsApp us, or fill in the form above. For larger projects we can arrange a site visit to take measurements and discuss your vision in person.
What is the payment process?
We require a 50% deposit to begin production. The remaining 50% is due upon delivery and installation. For larger full-home projects, flexible payment plans can be discussed.
Do you deliver and install?
Yes. Delivery and professional installation is included in all our packages within Kumasi. We also deliver across the Ashanti Region and other regions — delivery cost outside Kumasi will be included in your quote.
Can I bring my own design or sketch?
We love it when clients come with ideas! You can bring a sketch, a photo, a Pinterest board — anything that helps us understand your vision. We'll work with you to make it a reality.
