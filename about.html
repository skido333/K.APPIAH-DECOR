Who We Are

The K.Appiah Decor Story

K.Appiah Decor was founded with a single belief: every home deserves furniture that is not just functional, but truly beautiful. What started as a small workshop in Pankrono-Estate has grown into one of Kumasi's most trusted names in bespoke furniture.

Our founder, a passionate woodworker with over a decade of hands-on experience, built this business on the principles of quality, honesty, and customer satisfaction. Every piece that leaves our workshop is a reflection of that founding vision.

Today we serve hundreds of satisfied clients across the Ashanti Region and beyond — crafting kitchen cabinets, wardrobes, beds, TV stands, shelving, and fully custom furniture, all made to your exact specifications.

What Drives Us

Our Core Values

These are the principles we bring to every project, every client, and every piece of furniture we create.

🏆

Uncompromising Quality

We use only premium wood and materials. Every joint, finish, and fitting is inspected before delivery — because your furniture should last generations.

🤝

Client-First Approach

We listen first. Your needs, your space, and your budget guide every decision. We're not satisfied until you are completely happy with the result.

Bespoke Craftsmanship

Nothing is mass-produced. Every piece is built from scratch to your specifications — unique dimensions, unique finish, unique to you.

Honest Communication

Reliable Delivery

Responsible Sourcing
